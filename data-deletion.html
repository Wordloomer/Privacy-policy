Delete Your Word Loom Account

To delete your Word Loom account and all associated data:

  1. Open the Word Loom app
  2. Go to Settings/Account
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion

If you cannot access the app, please contact us at: [kaurhar0707@gmail.com] with your account email, and we'll process the deletion manually.
