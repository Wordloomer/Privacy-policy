Privacy Policy for Wordloom
Effective Date: [June 6, 2026]

Introduction
At The Word Company, we care about protecting the privacy of children who use our word game, word loom. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. This policy is written in simple language that children and parents can understand.
Important: This App is designed for players ages 8 and older. If you're under 18, please review this policy with your parent or guardian.

What Information We Collect
1. Information You Provide Directly
	•	Game Progress Data: We save your word game scores, levels completed, and achievements so you don't lose progress when you switch devices
	•	Optional Username: If you choose to create a username (not your real name), we store it to identify your game account
2. Information Collected Automatically
	•	Device Information: We collect your device type (Android phone/tablet), operating system version, and unique device identifiers (like Android ID) to ensure the game works properly
	•	Usage Data: We track how you use the game (which levels you play, how long you play) to improve the game experience
	•	IP Address: We collect your IP address for security purposes and to prevent fraud
3. We DO NOT Collect:
	•	❌ Your real name, address, or phone number
	•	❌ Your email address (unless you voluntarily contact us)
	•	❌ Photos, camera, or microphone access
	•	❌ Location data beyond what's needed for basic functionality
	•	❌ Contact lists or SMS information
	•	❌ Payment information (if you make purchases, it's handled by Google Play)

How We Protect Your Information
We take security seriously:
	•	🔒 Encryption: We use encryption to protect data when it's sent between your device and our servers
	•	🔒 Limited Access: Only our team members who need access to fix problems can see your data
	•	🔒 Secure Servers: Your information is stored on secure servers with password protection
	•	🔒 Regular Updates: We regularly update our security measures

How Long We Keep Your Data
	•	Game Progress: We keep your game data as long as you continue playing (typically up to 2 years)
	•	Device Information: We keep this for 6 months after you last play
	•	Usage Data: We keep this for 1 year to improve the game
You can request deletion of your data at any time (see "Contact Us" below)
